## Things I've made
 
### 🌌 [morganvanv.com](https://morganvanv.com)
 
My personal site, rebuilt after I got into the IndieWeb, the Web Revival, and neocities. Now it is slightly more interesting, and is functionally my replacement for social media:
 
- the sky behind every page is the **real sky**, drawn from actual star catalogs and astronomy math, with the Moon in its real phase
- a light is left on in the farmhouse after dark, and there's a campfire next to it
- you can name a star (a real one, and I'll see it)
- you navigate with the **arrow keys**, which are listed in my `humans.txt` under "Standards"
- there are no cookies, no trackers, and no evil algorithms
Built with Angular, and TypeScript. [Source is right here](https://github.com/morgan-vanv/morganvanv.com).
 
### 🏰 [Valkenburg Castle: The Heir's Return](https://morgan-vanv.github.io/Valkenburg-Castle/)
 
A turn-based extraction crawler for the browser, inspired by Task Force Games' 1980 *Valkenburg Castle*. Take contracts, come back alive. If Keven falls, Valkenburg stays dark. TypeScript, Three.js, Vite. [Play it](https://morgan-vanv.github.io/Valkenburg-Castle/) or [read the code](https://github.com/morgan-vanv/Valkenburg-Castle).
